The problem it solves

Free gyms are everywhere.
Finding a good one isn't.

Cities are full of outdoor bars, but nobody keeps a list of them. Regular maps don't show them, and when they do, they can't tell you whether the bars are still standing.

“Where can I train near here?”

Spots are hard to find

Outdoor gyms hide in parks, schoolyards and between blocks. You only know the ones you happen to walk past.

A map and a list of spots, sorted by distance from you.

“Is it worth the walk?”

You don't know what's there

A pin on a map says nothing about the equipment. You arrive and find one low bar, or broken rings.

Each spot lists its equipment, how many, condition and photos.

“Why keep going?”

Training alone fades

Without a coach or a gym membership, it's easy to skip a day, then a week.

XP, levels, daily streaks and a leaderboard to keep you showing up.

What's inside

Everything a street athlete needs

Map and list

Browse spots on a map or as a list with the nearest first. Filter by the equipment you need today.

Real spot details

Equipment, quantity and condition (good, worn or damaged), plus photos from people who trained there.

Ratings and reviews

Rate the spots you've trained at and read what others thought before you go.

Add a missing spot

Standing at a spot that isn't listed? Add it with your location and a photo in under a minute, and earn XP for it.

Train and level up

Start a workout at a spot, keep your daily streak alive, unlock achievements and climb the leaderboard.

Stay consistent

Every session counts

Outdoor training has no membership card pushing you to show up. OutGym gives you a reason to come back tomorrow.

  • XP for what you doWorkouts, new spots, photos and reviews all earn XP and move you up a level.
  • Daily streakA gentle reminder in the middle of the day keeps the streak from slipping.
  • Achievements and crownsCollect badges and defend your spots against other athletes.
  • Share your workoutPost a finished workout card to your friends in one tap.

Workout calendar

Your month at a glance

Every day you train lights up on the calendar. Days in a row join into one streak, so you see your rhythm and the gaps in it.

  • Streak and monthly totalDays in a row and this month's workouts sit right at the top.

How it works

From couch to bar in three steps

Open the map

Allow location and the nearest spots appear first. Location is used only while the app is open.

Pick your spot

Check the equipment, its condition, photos and reviews, then get directions.

Train and earn

Tap “Train here”, finish your session and collect XP. Rate the spot on your way out.

Get in touch

Questions, ideas or a spot we got wrong?

Write to us. Feedback from athletes shapes what we build next. Requests about your data are welcome here too; you can also delete your account at any time from Settings in the app.
